What these settle

No dashboard. Home is the Timeline. A summary screen aggregating five things you can already see is the personal-app feature that gets opened twice.

Capture appears on two screens, identically — Timeline and Achievements. It is the one element allowed to repeat, because the cost of it being one click away is the whole log.

Suppression over partial figures. Switch premium with one position renders as a sentence explaining what it needs, not as 0%. Atlas already sets this precedent and it matters more here, where the numbers are about someone's pay.

Login and first-run are still undesigned — deliberately, since they are the last thing built.
