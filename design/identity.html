The suite mark is one shared shape recoloured per app — Prometheus #e8935c and Atlas #4f8dff are byte-identical apart from the fill. So the accent is the logo, and it has to hold at 18px in a browser tab beside both of them. That rules out warm (Prometheus's) and blue (Atlas's), and it rules out anything pale: a washed accent reads as a colour nobody chose. Hyperion fathered the moon as well as the sun, so it takes Selene — moonlight, with enough chroma to be a mark.

Named for the concept rather than the pigment, the way Prometheus names its accent --fire and not --orange. Amethyst is the swatch; Selene is what it means. rise and fall stay deliberately muted so they cannot compete with it, and each colour carries a stated jurisdiction the way Prometheus's category palette does — a palette without one becomes fruit salad.

A fact, not a prompt. Nothing to dismiss, no cadence to get wrong.

The Pillar encodes something true rather than decorating: your career is continuous, and what varies is what you were doing inside it. A position thickens the rule, a gap thins it to a hairline, and overlapping positions split it into two strands that rejoin — which is exactly what contract work alongside a salaried job is, and it needs no special case. Marks crossing the rule are events; only selene reaches the current stretch, so "where am I now" is answered before you read a word. Hyperion is the Pillar of the East, and the rule is the pillar — which is the justification, and also why it is vertical.
