Accent options — with the mark

The suite mark is one shared shape recoloured per app — Prometheus and Atlas are byte-identical apart from the fill. So the accent is the logo, and it has to hold at 18px in a browser tab as well as it does on a page. Everything else here is held constant; only --accent moves.

Aurum

#E3B23C · saturated gold
favicon · 18px
the shelf test — three tabs, one glance

The mythologically exact one. Prometheus is firelight; Hyperion fathered Helios and is sunlight — the same family of light at a different order of magnitude. Highest chroma of the four and the strongest mark at 18px by some distance.

Risk: it is Prometheus's neighbour on the wheel, about 20° away. Whether that reads as “sibling” or as “I couldn't tell those two tabs apart” is the whole question, and the three-mark strip below is where to judge it.

favicon · 18px
the shelf test — three tabs, one glance

Selene's idea with the chroma it was missing. Hyperion fathered the moon as well as the sun, and this is moonlight with enough presence to be a logo. Clearly not Atlas's azure, clearly not Prometheus's fire — the cleanest separation of the four.

Risk: Atlas carries #8B5CF6 in its token file, so the hue is not wholly unspoken-for — though it is not Atlas's mark, which is what actually counts here.
